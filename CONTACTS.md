# 📇 Contacts

How to reach Psanctuary Church. Every address and channel here is one the church publishes on
[psanctuary.org](https://www.psanctuary.org) for this purpose; nobody's personal details are listed.
The same paths open as one window from **Reach out** on every Psanctuary Church page on GitHub.

*Last checked 2026-10-22.*

## ✉️ Write to the church

| Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [info@psanctuary.org](mailto:info@psanctuary.org) | The church's public address: membership, Sunday service, ceremonies, courses, giving (and receipts for gifts), or these pages |
| [The contact form](https://www.psanctuary.org/contact) | In the church's own words: "To reach our team, please fill out the form below or e-mail info@psanctuary.org." |

## 🍄 Where the members gather

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Psanctuary Member Community](https://psanctuary-member-community.mn.co/) | The church's member network on Mighty Networks: events, member-led clubs, courses and messages, on the web and in the Mighty app. One link: visitors see how to join, and members land on their dashboard. [psanctuary.org/membership](https://www.psanctuary.org/membership) lists the plans |

The church gathers its community in this one place on purpose, so there's no Discord.
